Prologue

"Some memories never leave... they just wait for us to return."

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat kahaniyan kisi bade shehar ya kisi heroic moment se nahi, balki ek school ke classroom se shuru hoti hain.

Ye kahani un dino ki hai jab dosti ka matlab sirf saath baithna nahi, balki bina kuch kahe ek doosre ko samajhna tha.

Jab school ki ghanti sirf period change hone ka signal nahi hoti thi, balki naye lamhon ki shuruaat bhi hoti thi.

Chapter 1 – The Beginning

📅 Monday | 10:00 AM

THE DAYS WE NEVER KNEW Book One – Part I PROLOGUE

"Some memories never leave... they just wait for us to return."

_____________________________________________
School kabhi sirf ek building nahi hota. Kabhi-kabhi woh hazaaron yaadon ka ghar hota hai. Ek aisi jagah... Jahan se nikalne ke baad bhi insaan kabhi poori tarah bahar nahi aa pata.

...

Kai saal baad...

School ka wahi purana gate uske saamne khada tha. Rang pehle jaisa chamakdar nahi raha tha. Deewaron par waqt ke nishan saaf dikh rahe the. Assembly ground pehle se chhota lag raha tha... Ya shayad woh khud bada ho gaya tha. Usne dheere se ek gehri saans li.

Mitti ki wohi khushboo... Jo bachpan se uske dil mein kahin chhup kar baithi thi. Gate ke paas khada chowkidar use pehchaan nahi paaya. Aur shayad pehchaan pata bhi kaise...

Waqt sirf chehre nahi badalta... Insaan bhi badal deta hai.

Woh dheere-dheere school ke andar chalne laga. Har kadam ke saath ek purani yaad laut rahi thi.

School corridor... Notice board... Water cooler... Aur phir...

Class IX

Darwaza aadha khula tha. Usne halka sa dhakka diya.

"Krrr..."

Purana lakdi ka darwaza aaj bhi waise hi awaaz karta tha. Classroom bilkul khaali tha. Benches wahi thi. Blackboard wahi tha. Khidkiyon se aati dhoop bhi lagbhag waise hi thi. Sirf ek cheez badal gayi thi...

Waqt.

Woh dheere se last bench ki taraf badha. Usne ungliyon se bench ki satah ko chhua. Lakdi par kuch purane naam kharoche hue the.

Kuch dikh rahe the... Kuch waqt ne mita diye the. Uske honton par ek halki si muskaan aayi. Aur usi pal... Ek purani notebook bench ke neeche se phisal kar zameen par gir gayi.

"Thak..."

Usne jhuk kar notebook uthai. Cover purana ho chuka tha. Konon se kagaz mur chuke the. Usne dheere se pehla panna khola. Bas ek hi naam likha tha...

Eliana....

Uska haath ek pal ke liye wahin ruk gaya. Aankhen band ho gayin. Aur saalon purani yaadein... Jaise kisi ne ek saath zinda kar di hon. Bahaar school ki bell baji. "Trinnnnnn..." Usne dheere se aankhen kholi. Aur pehli baar... Usne dil hi dil mein kaha— "Kuch kahaniyan khatam nahi hoti... woh bas yaadon mein chalti rehti hain." Notebook uske haath mein thi... Aur kahani... Wapas us din ja chuki thi... Jis din sab kuch shuru hua tha.
