Chapter 1

PART I

THE FIRST DAY

📅 Monday | 10:00 AM

"Kuch din normal lagte hain lekin baad mein wahi din zindagi ke sabse khaas panne ban jaate hain"

School gate ke bahar hamesha ki tarah bheed thi. Aur kuch naye session ke pehle din ko lekar excited the. Bas ek anjaan sa ehsaas. Dil mein ek ajeeb si feeling thi. Kuch apne purane doston ke saath baatein kar rahe the… Kuch bachche haste hue andar ja rahe the… Lekin mere liye… Main school gate ke paas kuch pal ke liye ruk gaya. Maine apna bag theek kiya aur school ke andar chala gaya. Na darr… Na khushi… Naye chehre. Naye log. Nayi class. Pata nahi kyun… Ye sab kuch naya tha.

Corridor mein bachchon ki awaazein goonj rahi thi. Har classroom ke bahar alag hi duniya bani hui thi. Kahin purane dost mil rahe the… Kahin naye dost ban rahe the. M ain apni class dhoondte hue aage badha. Classroom ke bahar rukkar ek baar andar dekha. Kuch students pehle se baithe hue the. Kuch baatein kar rahe the. Kuch apni notebooks arrange kar rahe the. Main dheere se andar gaya.

Classroom mein jaakar meri nazar sabse pehle benches par gayi. Kuch jagah groups mein log baithe the. Kuch seats khaali thi. Maine bina zyada soche ek seat choose ki. First bench. Shayad isliye kyunki pehle din main zyada attention nahi chahta tha. Ya shayad isliye… Kyuki mujhe abhi nahi pata tha ki meri asli jagah kahan hone wali thi. Maine bag rakha. Notebook nikali. Aur chup-chaap baith gaya.

Thodi der baad class teacher andar aayi. Sab bachche khade ho gaye. “Good Morning Ma’am.” “Good Morning. Sit down.” Class shaant ho gayi. Teacher ne attendance register khola. Ek-ek karke naam bulaye jaane lage. Har naam ke saath ek awaaz aati— “Present Ma’am.” Naye class ke pehle din ki woh feeling alag hi hoti hai. Har koi ek dusre ko dekh raha hota hai… Lekin koi pehla kadam nahi uthata.

Main apni notebook ke page ko dekh raha tha. Main apni notebook ke page ko dekh raha tha. Tabhi peeche se halki si hasi ki awaaz aayi. Maine mudkar dekha. Last bench par kuch bachche baithe the. Unke chehre par ek alag hi confidence tha. Na kisi baat ki tension… Na kisi se zyada fark. Bas apni hi duniya mein khush. Unmein se ek ladka sabse zyada baat kar raha tha. Woh har baat ko funny bana deta tha. Class ke kuch bachche uski baaton par muskura rahe the. Main bas dekh raha tha. Mujhe uska naam nahi pata tha… Lekin us din pehli baar maine use notice kiya.

Pehla period shuru hua. Teacher board par likhne lagi. Main poori koshish kar raha tha ki dhyan laga kar padhun. Lekin naye classroom ka mahaul samajhne mein bhi waqt lag raha tha. Kaun kaisa hai… Kaun kis ke saath hai… Kaun sirf dikhne mein shaant hai aur asal mein kitna shararti… Ye sab dheere-dheere pata chalna tha.

Kuch der baad teacher ne ek question poocha. Class bilkul shaant ho gayi. Tabhi peeche se wahi awaaz aayi— “Ma’am… answer toh pata hai, bas bolne se pehle himmat collect kar raha hoon.” Poore classroom mein hasi phail gayi. Teacher ne peeche dekha. “Himmat jaldi collect kar lo, warna answer kisi aur ko dena padega.” Woh ladka chup ho gaya… Lekin uski smile nahi gayi.

Main apni notebook par wapas dhyan dene laga. Mujhe nahi pata tha… Ki jis ladke ko main sirf ek funny classmate samajh raha tha… Wahi kuch waqt baad meri school life ka sabse important dost banne wala tha. Aur jis classroom ko main sirf ek nayi jagah samajh raha tha… Wahi jagah meri zindagi ki sabse khoobsurat yaadon ka hissa banne wali thi.

Time: 12:00 PM

Lunch bell bajne wali thi… Aur mujhe bilkul nahi pata tha… Ki agle kuch ghanton mein meri ek aisi dosti shuru hone wali thi… Jo sirf ek school friendship nahi rehne wali thi.
