PART II

THE FIRST DAY

📅 Monday | 10:00 AM

"Kabhi-kabhi ek nayi jagah par sabse pehla apnapan ek chhoti si baat se milta hai aur wahi chhoti si baat ek badi kahani ki shuruat ban jaati hai."

Pehla din tha Aur har cheez thodi alag lag rahi thi. Nayi class. Naye chehre. Nayi books. Aur ek nayi shuruaat. Main abhi bhi first bench par baitha tha. Meri notebook khuli hui thi Lekin sach kahun toh aadha dhyan padhai mein aur aadha dhyan classroom ko samajhne mein tha. Kaun kis type ka hai Kaun kis se baat karta hai Aur kaun sirf shor karta hai Sab kuch dheere-dheere samajh aa raha tha.

Teacher board par padha rahi thi. Class mein ek ajeeb si khamoshi thi. Sirf chok ki awaaz aur pages palatne ki awaaz sunai de rahi thi. Tabhi peeche se halki si hansi ki awaaz aayi. Main ek baar phir mudkar dekha! Last bench par wahi group baitha tha. Unki duniya alag hi chal rahi thi. Teacher padhati Aur woh log apni chhoti-chhoti baaton mein busy rehte. Unmein ek ladka tha Jo har situation mein kuch funny bol deta tha. Uski wajah se aas-paas baithe bachche bhi muskura dete.

Ek baar teacher ne board par question likha.Iska answer kaun dega? Poora class chup ho gaya. Sab apni notebooks dekhne lage. Tabhi peeche se awaaz aayiMaam answer toh aata hai, bas pehle ye decide kar raha hoon ki bolna hai ya chup rehna hai Class mein halki hasi phail gayi. Teacher ne uski taraf dekha.Tum decide karte-karte period khatam kar doge Woh ladka muskura diya. Maam, koshish jaari hai Is baar teacher bhi halka sa smile kar di.

Mujhe nahi pata kyun. Lekin us ladke ki ek alag hi energy thi. Woh un logon mein se tha jo jahan hote hain wahan ki duniya ko thoda aur interesting bana dete hain. Wahan thodi hasi zaroor hoti hai. Lekin abhi tak hamari koi baat nahi hui thi. Bas ek doosre ko notice kiya tha.

Time dheere-dheere aage badhta gaya. Ek ke baad ek period khatam hote gaye. Kabhi Maths... Kabhi English... to Kabhi Science..... Pehle din ki classes thodi lambi lag rahi thi. Shayad isliye kyunki sab kuch naya tha.

Main apni notebook band kar hi raha tha ki clock par nazar gayi..
