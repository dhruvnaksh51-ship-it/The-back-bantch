Chapter 1

PART III

Date: 05 August

Timing: 12:05

Location: School Ground

“Kuch mulaqatein bas ek din ka hissa hoti hain… aur kuch mulaqatein poori zindagi ka matlab badal deti hain.”

Lunch bell bajte hi poora classroom khaali hone laga. Sab apne-apne doston ke saath bahar nikal gaye. Kisi ke haath mein tiffin tha… Kisi ke chehre par hasi…
Aurkisi ke paas lunch se zyada baatein thi. Main apna tiffin lekar dheere-dheere classroom se bahar nikla. Pehla din tha… Isliye abhi tak koi aisa nahi tha jiske saath main bina soche baith jaun. Main ground ke ek kone ki taraf badha hi tha ki peeche se awaaz aayi—
“Oye… ruk.” Main mudkar dekha. Wahi ladka… Jo subah last bench par sabko hasa raha tha. Woh haste hue mere paas aaya. “Tu naya hai na?” Maine sir hilaya. “Haan.”
Woh muskuraya. “Toh phir akele lunch kyun? Chal saath mein khate hain.” Maine kuch nahi kaha… Bas halka sa smile kiya aur uske saath chal diya.

Hum ground ke ek kone mein baith gaye. Usne apna tiffin khola. “Waise naam toh bata.” Maine usse apna naam bataya. Phir usne apna haath aage badhaya. “Kabir.” Maine uska haath pakda. Uski personality pehli mulaqat mein hi alag thi. Aisa lag raha tha jaise woh kisi se bhi baat kar sakta hai.

Kabir ne apna tiffin khola. “Rule number one…” Main uski taraf dekhne laga. “Dosti ki shuruaat tiffin share karne se hoti hai.”

Main hans diya. “Ye rule kis book mein likha hai?” Kabir ne bina soche jawab diya— “Meri book mein.” Dono has pade. Pata nahi kyun… Lekin us chhoti si baat mein bhi ek apnapan tha.

Lunch ke baad hum school ke andar ghoomne lage. Lunch ke baad hum school ke andar ghoomne lage. Kabir mujhe har jagah ke baare mein batata ja raha tha.

“Ye library hai…” “Ye ground hai…” “Ye wala corner teachers se bachne ke kaam aata hai.” Main uski baatein sun kar bas muskura raha tha. Subah tak jo school anjaan lag raha tha… Ab thoda apna sa lagne laga tha.

Time: 1:30 PM

Lunch break khatam hone ki bell baj gayi. Hum dono classroom ki taraf wapas chale. Kabir ruk gaya. Usne last bench ki taraf dekha. Phir meri taraf. “Chal… ab tujhe asli jagah dikhata hoon.” Main thoda confuse hua. “Asli jagah?” Woh hasa. “Haan… first bench se zindagi nahi samajh aati.”

Hum dono last bench par jaakar baith gaye. Wahan baithe sab log meri taraf dekhne lage. Kabir ne smile karte hue kaha— “Guys, ye hai hamara naya dost.” Ek ladke ne haste hue kaha— “Welcome to the backbench.”

Dusre ne bola— “Yahan padhai kam aur yaadein zyada banti hain.” Sab hans pade. Ek-ek karke sabne apna introduction diya. Kisi ka nature funny tha… Kisi ka shaant… Kisi ka hamesha help karne wala. Aur phir ek ladki ne halka sa smile karke kaha—

“Hi… I’m Eliana.” Maine sirf halka sa smile kiya. “Hi.” Bas itni si baat hui. Us waqt mujhe nahi pata tha… Ki kabhi-kabhi ek chhota sa introduction bhi zindagi ki sabse badi kahani ka pehla page hota hai.

Time: 4:00 PM

School ki last bell baj chuki thi. Sab apne bags lekar ghar jaane lage. Kabir ne jaate-jaate kaha— “Kal first bench par mat baith jana.” Main hasa. “Kyun?” Woh muskuraya. “Kyuki ab teri seat yahan hai.” Usne last bench ki taraf ishara kiya. Main ek baar peeche mudkar classroom ko dekha. Subah tak jo jagah anjaan thi… Shaam tak wahi jagah thodi apni lagne lagi thi. Mujhe nahi pata tha… Ki ye sirf ek school day nahi tha. Ye meri zindagi ke un dino ki shuruaat thi… Jinhe main kabhi bhool nahi paunga.
