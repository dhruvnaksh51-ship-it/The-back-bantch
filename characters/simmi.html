SIMMI

Sister by Heart • The Silent Strength of the Group

Biography

Simmi is the Hero's sister by heart and one of the most caring people in The Days We Never Knew. She has a warm personality that makes everyone feel comfortable around her. She is intelligent, kind, understanding, and always ready to support the people she loves. Although Simmi enjoys laughing and spending time with her friends, she is also a great listener. Whenever someone is sad or confused, she is the first person to notice and offer comfort. Her advice often helps her friends overcome difficult situations. Simmi loves reading novels, writing in her diary, singing, and spending peaceful evenings at cafés while talking with her friends. She believes that true happiness comes from honest relationships and unforgettable memories rather than material things. She has a special sibling-like bond with the Hero. Even though they are not related by blood, they trust each other completely and always stand by one another during life's happiest and hardest moments. Throughout the story, Simmi plays an important role in keeping the group together. Her kindness, wisdom, and positive nature make her one of the emotional pillars of the story.
