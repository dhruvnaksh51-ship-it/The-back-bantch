ELIANA

The First Love • The Light in Every Memory

Biography

Eliana is one of the most important characters in The Days We Never Knew. She is cheerful, kind-hearted, and always carries a calm smile that makes people around her feel comfortable. Although she looks confident, she has her own fears, dreams, and emotions that she rarely shares with anyone. She first meets the Hero during school life, and their friendship grows naturally through everyday conversations, shared memories, and countless small moments. What begins as a simple friendship slowly becomes one of the most meaningful relationships in both their lives. Eliana believes that memories are more valuable than expensive gifts. She enjoys spending time with her friends, capturing beautiful moments through photography, and listening to music that matches her mood. She is intelligent, honest, and always tries to help others before thinking about herself. Her positive attitude often brings hope to people around her, making her someone everyone enjoys being with. Even during difficult times, Eliana chooses kindness over anger and hope over fear. Her journey is filled with laughter, emotional moments, and unforgettable memories that shape the story.
